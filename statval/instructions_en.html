In this study, we will show you various answers that people gave when they were asked to describe themselves in 2-3 sentences, by, e.g. mentioning their your hobbies, interests, family, etc. These individuals have participated in a previous survey

Your task is to evaluate how stereotypically female or male you find each description. This is not about guessing whether the description is from a woman or a man, but about judging how strongly the description reflects typical female or male characteristics. There is no “correct” answer – we are only interested in your personal impression of each description.
