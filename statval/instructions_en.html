In this study, we will show you various answers that people when they were asked to describe themselves in 2-3 sentences, by, e.g. mentioning their your hobbies, interests, family, etc. These individuals have participated in a previous survey

Your task is to evaluate how stereotypically female or male you find each introduction. There is no “correct” answer – we are only interested in your personal impression of each introduction.
